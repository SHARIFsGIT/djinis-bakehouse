# Launch checklist — opening the shop to real customers

Written 2026-11-22 for the release on the branch
`payments/unified-ledger-paypal`. Updated 2026-11-23: that release is
deployed (step 2 below is done); what is still open is listed, with the fix
for each, in `PENDING.md` in the repository root. Every other step here
remains the owner's to take. Payments have their own
detailed runbook, `PAYMENTS_GO_LIVE.md`; this page is the whole launch in
order, and points there for the payment steps.

The shop can open in stages. Cash on delivery needs no provider at all;
cards follow once Stripe has approved the account; PayPal is optional.
Nothing in the list below asks for a real payment as a test.

---

## 1. What you need, and where it comes from

No key is needed to run the shop on your own machine. Production needs the
ones below. Every value goes into Render → the service **djinis-bakehouse**
→ **Environment** — never into a file in this repository.

### Already in place (the Render blueprint creates or carries them)

| Setting | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `APP_ENV=production` | switches on the production guards |
| `SECRET_KEY` | signs logins; generated by Render — never share or reuse it |
| `DATABASE_URL` | the managed PostgreSQL **bakehouse-db** |
| `ADMIN_EMAIL` / `ADMIN_PASSWORD` | the first admin, created on an empty database only |
| `TAX_RATE`, `SHIPPING_FEE`, `FREE_SHIPPING_THRESHOLD`, `VIP_FREE_DELIVERY_THRESHOLD` | the approved money rules (7 %, €2.99, free from €25 / €15 for VIPs) |
| `UPLOADS_DIR=/data/uploads` | photos on the persistent disk |

### To set before opening

| Setting | Needed for | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_USER`, `SMTP_PASSWORD`, `SMTP_FROM` (and `SMTP_PORT` only if it is not 587 — 465 works too) | **every email the shop sends**: receipts, order updates, password resets, back-in-stock notes, desk alerts. Without them nothing is sent. | An SMTP account from a mail provider or your domain host. Send from an address on your own domain and publish the SPF and DKIM records the provider gives you, or receipts land in spam. |
| `PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://djinisbakehouse.com` | links in emails and the return from a pay page always carry the real domain | Your domain. The shop already answers on this one address (`www` and `http` redirect to it). |
| `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` (`sk_live_…`), `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` (`whsec_…`) | card payments, wallets, and whatever else is switched on in Stripe | dashboard.stripe.com, once Stripe has verified the business. The webhook endpoint and its eight events: `PAYMENTS_GO_LIVE.md`, step 4. |

### Optional

| Setting | Adds | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID`, `PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET`, `PAYPAL_ENVIRONMENT=live`, `PAYPAL_WEBHOOK_ID` | PayPal as its own option at checkout | developer.paypal.com → Apps & Credentials → a **Live** REST app (needs a PayPal Business account). `PAYMENTS_GO_LIVE.md`, steps 3 and 5. |
| `FACEBOOK_APP_ID`, `FACEBOOK_APP_SECRET` | "Continue with Facebook" | developers.facebook.com — README, "Turning on Facebook and phone sign-in" |
| `BIRD_API_KEY` (a secret) | sign-in codes by SMS through Bird Verify — customers confirm their number first; admins never sign in by text | bird.com dashboard → API keys (EU workspace, Verify read/write) — same README section. Deleting it switches text sign-in off again |
| `SHOP_TIMEZONE` | the bakery's clock, if it is not `Europe/Berlin` | an IANA zone name |
| `CLIENT_IP_HEADER` | rate limits keyed on the address your edge reports | only after confirming which header the edge sets itself — see `backend/.env.example` |
| `TAX_MODE=inclusive` | VAT-inclusive shelf prices | not before every product carries an approved VAT classification — README, "Turning on VAT-inclusive pricing" |

Keys have a mode. `sk_test_…` and the PayPal sandbox rehearse without real
money (checkout says so on the page); `sk_live_…` and PayPal live charge.
Test and live webhooks have different secrets.

## 2. The launch, in order

Each step ends with something to look at. Stop at the first one that does
not look right.

1. **Back up the production database and check the backup.**
   `PAYMENTS_GO_LIVE.md`, step 0. The release only adds tables and two
   empty columns, but no change reaches a database with real orders
   without a backup you have checked.

2. **Deploy the release.** Merge `payments/unified-ledger-paypal` into
   `main`; Render builds and deploys `main` by itself.
   *Look at:* the deploy log ends without a traceback and with a line
   naming the tills (`Payments: …`); `https://djinisbakehouse.com/api/health`
   answers with `"status":"ok"`; the sign-up page (`/register`) carries the
   new line "By creating an account you accept our terms" under its button.

3. **Set the environment values from section 1** (`PUBLIC_BASE_URL`, the
   `SMTP_*` set). Render restarts the service on save.
   *Look at:* the boot log no longer says "Email is OFF"; use "forgot
   password" on your own account and the email arrives with a link that
   starts with `https://djinisbakehouse.com/`.

4. **Fill in the shop at the desk** (`/admin` → Settings):
   - *Contact & hours* — address, phone, the email customers may write to.
   - *Legal notice* — your Impressum, in your own words (see section 3).
   - *Delivery area* — the postal codes you deliver to, or leave it empty
     to accept any address.
   - *Storefront copy* — the announcement bar and the "today" note.

   Then Products: prices, stock, photos, **ingredients and allergens** of
   every bake, checked against the real recipes. And Promotions: which
   vouchers are live, and their minimum baskets.
   *Look at:* the footer shows your contact lines and a "Legal notice"
   link; checkout shows "We deliver to postal codes …" if you set an area.

5. **Run the readiness check** in the Render shell:

   ```bash
   python backend/scripts/check_payments.py
   ```

   It only reads. It ends with "Nothing blocks online payments" or with
   the list of what does, and it warns about anything around the payments
   that is still missing (email off, no legal notice, no public address).

6. **Rehearse, then switch the payment keys to live** —
   `PAYMENTS_GO_LIVE.md`, steps 2–5: a test card and a sandbox PayPal
   buyer first, a refund from the desk, then the live keys and the live
   webhooks. Keep cash on delivery on throughout.

7. **Clear out rehearsal data** if the database holds test customers and
   test orders: `PRODUCTION_RESET_PROCEDURE.md`. That procedure deletes
   data and is the owner's alone to run, after its own backup.

8. **Open.** Watch the first genuine order from the desk: Orders shows it,
   the payment panel names the provider's own payment id, the customer's
   receipt arrives, and Orders → Payment check lists nothing.

**If a deploy misbehaves:** Render → the service → Deploys → roll back to
the previous deploy. The new tables and columns stay where they are,
unused. (One limit: once a PayPal order exists, switch PayPal off instead
of rolling the code back — `PAYMENTS_GO_LIVE.md`, "Rolling back".)

## 3. Decisions that are yours, not the software's

The shop does not answer these; a tax adviser or lawyer does.

1. **Legal notice (Impressum).** What it must contain for your business.
   The page and its footer link exist only once you have written it.
2. **Privacy, Terms and Refunds pages.** They describe how the site
   behaves; have them reviewed against the rules that apply to you —
   in particular the payment processors named, and the right of
   withdrawal for fresh food.
3. **VAT.** Prices are currently net with a 7 % register rate added at the
   till (`TAX_MODE=exclusive`). Whether to show VAT-inclusive prices, and
   each product's rate, is a classification only you can approve. Your
   VAT number belongs in the Stripe dashboard's tax settings.
4. **Food information.** Ingredients and allergens are shown exactly as
   entered on each product. They must be true of what you bake.
5. **What the pages promise.** The product page says "Order by 2 p.m. —
   it's on the evening delivery run, or ready at the counter from 7 a.m.",
   and the home page speaks of the morning bake. Make sure the bakery's
   day really runs that way, or tell the developer the real times.
6. **Newsletter sign-ups.** The footer form stores an address when it is
   typed; no confirmation email is sent. Whether your newsletter tool
   should ask each address to confirm first (double opt-in) is a question
   for whoever advises you on e-marketing law.
7. **Vouchers.** `WELCOME10` and `FRESHBAKE15` are restored at every start
   because the storefront copy promises them. Check their terms (the
   minimum basket on `FRESHBAKE15`) under Promotions before opening.

## 4. Once the shop is open

- **Every morning:** Orders (new and unpaid), Orders → Payment check
  (anything listed there needs a look), the low-stock list on the
  Dashboard.
- **Refunds** go through the desk's Refund button, never by relabelling
  an order — the provider sends the money and confirms it.
- **Disputes and chargebacks** are answered in the Stripe dashboard or
  PayPal's Resolution Centre, before their deadline. No setup prevents
  every chargeback or fraudulent payment.
- **Backups:** Render keeps snapshots of the managed database; know where
  they are and how a restore is started before you need one.
- **The shop runs as one process.** Its rate limits live in that process;
  that is right for this size. Before running several instances, the
  limits need a shared store.
- **Secrets** stay in Render's environment. If one is ever pasted
  somewhere it should not be, replace it at the provider and update
  Render — a rotated `SECRET_KEY` signs everyone out, nothing more.
